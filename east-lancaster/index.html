What East Lancaster Means Here

East Lancaster is the east side of the city, and it is named in several local and real estate references. We place it by landmarks, using the addresses in Wikipedia's list of Lancaster parks. The James C. Gilley Lancaster National Soccer Center is at 43000 30th Street East, Tierra Bonita Park is at 44910 27th Street East, Deputy Pierre W. Housing mix

Homes of Different Ages, Close Together

The same listing-site guide describes midcentury ranch homes on the side closer to central Lancaster, newer two-story tracts farther east, and condos, townhouses and apartments in the mix. That range is the point of this page. Two houses a few blocks apart can have different plumbing, and a house's age does not tell us what is inside the walls or under it. Pipe materials, water heater age and past repairs vary home by home, and we do not assume them.

A repipe makes sense in some homes and not in others. See our repiping page for the signs that suggest it, and ask us before you commit to anything based on a neighbor's experience.

Know what you have

Three Quick Checks Before You Call

First, find the main shutoff valve and make sure everyone in the house knows where it is. Second, look at the exposed pipe near the water heater. Copper is reddish brown, galvanized steel is gray with threaded fittings, and PEX is flexible plastic in red, blue or white. Tell us what you see, but treat it as a clue and not a diagnosis. Third, look at the label on your water heater. Many labels carry a serial number that encodes the manufacture date, and the manufacturer can tell you how to read it.

These checks are general plumbing guidance and not a statement about what is common in East Lancaster. Our water heater repair page explains when a tank is worth repairing and when replacement makes more sense.

Slow drain or a gurgling toilet? Describe it when you call and we will tell you what to try first.

Drains and trees

Drains, Roots and Yard Work

Mature trees near a sewer lateral can be a cause of recurring backups, and that is general plumbing information and not a claim about any one street. If a drain backs up more than once in the same place, a camera look is the fastest way to find out why. Our drain cleaning page describes the approach. If you plan to dig, call 811 first through DigAlert.

For the sewer systems it maintains, LA County Public Works says the lateral is owned entirely by the property owner, including the part under the street. (source) Confirm which agency serves your street before you plan a repair.
