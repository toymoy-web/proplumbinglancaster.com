Where We Work

Lancaster is our home base, and where the majority of calls come from - a mix of newer construction and 1970s-80s slab-foundation homes across the sewered core of the city.

Palmdale sits just south of Lancaster and shares the same Antelope Valley groundwater and high-desert climate, so the same hard-water and slab-leak patterns apply.

Quartz Hill is the unincorporated community directly southwest of Lancaster, with a similar mix of housing ages to Lancaster's own older neighborhoods.

Rosamond lies about 11 miles north of Lancaster, just across the Kern County line near Edwards Air Force Base - still the same Antelope Valley water supply and desert conditions.

Leona Valley and Antelope Acres are smaller, more rural communities on Lancaster's outer edge - Antelope Acres in particular is largely outside the public sewer district, which is where our septic tank pumping calls concentrate.
